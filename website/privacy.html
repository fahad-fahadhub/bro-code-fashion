Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

This page explains what Bro Code Fashion does with the details you enter on this website.

What we collect

  • When you check out, you type your name, mobile number, delivery address, city, pincode and optional notes.
  • Your bag and these details are kept in your own browser so you do not have to retype them. They are not stored on any server by this website.

How we use it

Your details are used to write the WhatsApp order message that you send to us. We use them to confirm your order, arrange payment and delivery, and reply to your questions.

WhatsApp

When you send your order, WhatsApp (Meta) handles the message under its own privacy policy. Messages go to our business number +91 82968 89214.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set tracking cookies. If we switch on analytics later (for example Google Analytics or Meta Pixel), we will update this page.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser data to remove the bag and saved details from your device.
  • Message us on WhatsApp if you want us to delete the details in an order conversation.
