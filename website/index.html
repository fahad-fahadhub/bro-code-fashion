Questions

Good to know.

How do I place an order?

Open a tee, choose your size and add it to your bag. In checkout, enter your delivery details and tap Confirm order. WhatsApp opens with your full order, and you press send.

Do I pay on the website?

No. This website does not take payments. We confirm your order, the total and the payment details with you on WhatsApp.

What are your hours?

You can order any time. We reply on WhatsApp between 9 AM and 9 PM.

How much is delivery and when will it arrive?

We confirm delivery availability, charges and timing for your pincode on WhatsApp before you pay.

What if I am not sure about my size?

Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose before you order.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Return and exchange terms are shared on WhatsApp before you pay. See our Shipping & returns page for details.

Is there a store I can visit?

We are based in HSR Layout, Bengaluru and are building towards a store. For now, orders are placed online and confirmed on WhatsApp.
